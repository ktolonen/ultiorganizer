Finnish Flying Disc Association's Ultiorganizer is an Ultimate scoring system, where you can find game schedules, results and scoring leaders. Scoring system also includes all the statistics from Finnish national league played on this millennium.

What do you want to do?

  • Get familiar with Ultiorganizer? Check links on left navigation pane. There is no need for registration to access data.
  • See division status? Click the name of the division or pool you want to examine.
  • Check your favorite team scores? Find the team under Teams link.
  • Find out player statistics? Browse all players or find the player from team roster or scoring leaders.
  • Manage your team data? Log in with team account.
  • Enroll your team into division or add media files? Register into Ultiorganizer by clicking "new user?" -link.
